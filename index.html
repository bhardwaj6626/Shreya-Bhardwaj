Try these before you buy. First, the formula behind every good prompt. Use as many parts as you have time for, and always include grade level and format.

PartWhat it doesExample
RoleWho the AI should act asYou are a 5th-grade math teacher.
ContextThe situation and audienceMy students just learned to add unlike fractions.
TaskExactly what you needWrite 8 multiple-choice questions.
ConstraintsLimits and rulesUse everyday examples; keep each question to two sentences.
FormatHow it should lookTable with an answer key.

Then three prompts from the book. Replace anything in [brackets] with your own details, and leave student names out.

Lesson hook

You are an experienced [grade] [subject] teacher. Create a 5-minute hook for a lesson on [topic] that sparks curiosity and needs no special materials.

Parent email

Write a warm, professional email to a parent about missing homework. Include two supportive next steps and keep it under 150 words. Do not use names.
